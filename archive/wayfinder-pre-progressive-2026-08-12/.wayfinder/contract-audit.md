## Expansion conflicts and required boundaries

1. **Stage-name collision.** Handoff “Phase 4” is prototype search/filter UI, while authoritative R4 is named local BOMs. Handoff “Phase 5” is a discovery direction, while authoritative R5 is launch. Do not map, mark, or report handoff phases as R4/R5 completion; rename or explicitly namespace them before execution tracking.
2. **v1 family scope.** The restart plan limits v1 and its 25 reviewed mappings to socket-head cap screws. Three-family search can be prototype-only; it cannot expand the verified-equivalent pilot, McMaster lookup corpus, or production catalog without a reviewed contract/readiness-packet change.
3. **Prototype provenance wording.** The contract permits *validated* hex-head and rounded-head local prototype configurations, non-published and excluded from generated routes. The handoff calls all three sets “generated.” Before relying on the allowance, retain/review provenance showing the added families are validated standards-defined configurations. Otherwise label them experimental generated candidates, not validated catalog data.
4. **Publication/commercial boundary.** The handoff correctly excludes static part/embed pages and commercial fields. Keep this true for lazy chunks, search results, labels, supplier handoffs, sitemap, and generated routes: configurations are never stocked/listed/equivalent/approved, and supplier URLs remain independent-verification search handoffs.
5. **Source gate is not satisfied.** “McMaster/Octopart-style” must not imply McMaster, Octopart, supplier listings, or acquired external records. No approved source record exists, and the contract prohibits scraping, unsanctioned bulk ingestion, prices, inventory, lead times, and supplier-listing claims.

1. Preserve/reset and use the new isolated worktree (R0 gate); do not advance the live checklist merely for handoff work.

5. Verify filter correctness, family isolation, static-route exclusion, absence of commercial claims, bundle size, keyboard/mobile behavior, typecheck, tests, build, and browser smoke.

Completion of these steps is not R4 (named BOM) completion and creates no production catalog expansion.

1. R0: recovery manifest, clean isolated baseline, reconciled reviewed code, clean verification, independent reset review.
2. R1: source audit; select a source recorded as `approved`; retain written commercial collection/storage/transformation/public-display permission plus permitted fields, attribution, refresh, takedown, and lawful 25-record pilot; independent clearance review.
3. R2: canonical configuration/source/evidence/lifecycle schemas; disabled-by-default adapter; normalization, validation/conflict/review queue; correction/withdrawal/versioning; publish only independently reviewed verified equivalents; independent pipeline review.
4. R3: exact McMaster lookup against the published catalog, evidence/review revision/alternative identifier, and explicit unsupported result; candidates stay separate; independent lookup review.
5. Only then implement the handoff discovery UI, preserving freshness, attribution, correction, withdrawal, and takedown controls. It still may not show price, inventory, lead time, supplier listing, unreviewed cross-reference, equivalence, or approved-alternate claims.
