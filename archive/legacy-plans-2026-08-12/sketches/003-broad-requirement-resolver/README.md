## Variant: Broad requirement resolver

### Design stance
Show the preserved requirement and unresolved family before any configuration rows.

### Key choices
- No dashboard or permanent sidebar.
- Broad query becomes explicit constraint tokens.
- Family candidates explain geometry and why they match.
- Selection reveals the next required decision.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: ambiguity, terminology, honest search interpretation.
- Weak at: expert exact lookup and dense comparison.

### Best for
Broad descriptions such as `M4 screw`.
