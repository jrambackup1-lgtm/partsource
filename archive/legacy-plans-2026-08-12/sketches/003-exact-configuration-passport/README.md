## Variant: Exact configuration passport

### Design stance
Known identifiers open evidence and configuration truth first, not a generic filter workspace.

### Key choices
- Identifier-to-configuration mapping is explicit.
- Direct, normalized, and missing facts stay distinct.
- Field rows open evidence details.
- Supplier searches remain separate and qualified.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: confidence, auditability, exact lookup.
- Weak at: broad exploration and quick family comparison.

### Best for
Known catalog identifiers such as `91290A115`.
