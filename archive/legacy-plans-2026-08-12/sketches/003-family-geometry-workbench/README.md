## Variant: Family geometry workbench

### Design stance
Use geometry and required dimensions to guide configuration while preserving a dense result table.

### Key choices
- Persistent requirement strip.
- Dimensioned family drawing leads.
- Required selectors filter published configurations.
- One unique configuration activates the inspector and BOM action.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: family-specific intent and learning selector meaning.
- Weak at: small screens and families without good diagrams.

### Best for
Specific intent such as `M4 socket head cap screw`.
