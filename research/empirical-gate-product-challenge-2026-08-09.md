# Empirical Gate Product Challenge — 2026-08-09

**Scope:** Empirical/domain validation only. This is not a specification, implementation plan, mechanical approval, release approval, or production-change authorization.  
**Gate recommendation:** **NO-GO**. Do not enter `/to-spec`.

## Executive gate

The internal work supports a **truth-preserving state-machine research contract**, not a validated product:

- preserve raw clues and explicit facts;
- distinguish exact, partial, conflict, unsupported, unknown, unavailable, and failure states;
- abstain rather than manufacture a unique selection;
- separate identifier mapping, configuration, release, supplier destination, and suitability;
- freeze an issue/configuration packet without requiring a supplier click; and
- use A's line resolver under any future batch shell.

It does **not** support direct-user value, a chosen commercial wedge, a reviewed fastener release, supplier-query handoff, BOM readiness, bearing coverage, or a broad mechanical platform.

**Product decision:**

1. **A remains only the least-unsupported research direction, not an approved or proven direction.** It earns one final manual recent-work test because B depends on its resolver and C's unchanged model has been falsified.
2. **B must not replace A.** The internal duel showed better packet structure but no better line truth, no reduction in substantive line reviews, weak grouping leverage, and no participant preference or value evidence.
3. **Shrink the next POC-shaped test** to a manual, non-production fastener clue/line → correction-aware issue packet. Remove alternate-supplier translation from the core completion claim. Do not claim three mechanically reviewed families, a ready BOM, or a platform.
4. **Stop specification and implementation work now.** Continue only the bounded participant and qualified-domain-review gate. If that gate cannot be executed or hits a kill criterion, choose D and stop the product direction rather than making another prototype.

## What the completed internal validation actually supports

1. **A safe line-state contract is feasible as a fixture composer.** The 120/120 result is self-consistency against a frozen contract, not an independent truth score.
2. **A is the required kernel beneath B.** Both conditions produced identical states, 46/46 declared critical-fact visibility, zero false unique fixture matches, and zero supported handoffs.
3. **B is at most a thin wrapper.** It improved the declared artifact rubric from 9/12 to 12/12 and reduced review starts from 10 to 8, but still required 10 substantive row reviews and added grouping bookkeeping.
4. **The frozen packet is safer than the supplier click.** The transparent fixture packet retained 8/8 declared facts; the current generic outgoing query retained 5/8, and release-aware handoff failed.
5. **The current fastener object model is not category-neutral.** Rolling-bearing desk evidence requires manufacturer product/execution and application-requirement targets distinct from a seller-neutral configuration.
6. **The candidate release has actionable defects.** Reported defects include low-profile leakage into the socket-head pool, invalid pitch/unit representation, lossy material/finish/strength projection, unresolved family boundaries, and absent immutable release/correction state.

## A/B/C/D challenge matrix

### A — fastener specification recovery and frozen issue/configuration packet

**Pass criteria before any `/to-spec` recommendation**

- At least five qualifying sessions (aim 6–8), each using permitted participant-owned recent work and an independently frozen answer key.
- Zero critical false unique matches, zero silent changes/drops/defaults of selection-critical facts, zero stale-result acceptance, and raw line identity retained in every counted task.
- More than half of qualifying participants produce a useful frozen artifact or a materially improved real next action; more than half name a concrete comparable reuse event.
- The normal source/catalog workflow is not consistently faster with equal or better keyed correctness for more than half.
- Locked teach-back distinguishes mapping, configuration, release, approval, supplier search, equivalence, and suitability without a workflow-induced unsafe action.
- Any selection/release claim is limited to a qualified reviewer-approved family profile and answer key tied to an immutable release digest. A pure unresolved issue packet may be tested without claiming selection.

**Fail criteria**

- Fewer than five qualifying sessions leaves the gate blocked; it is not a pass by anecdote.
- Fewer than half obtain a useful artifact or changed next action, or fewer than half can name plausible reuse.
- The packet is no clearer or more useful than opening the original source, even if fixture correctness remains green.
- No lawful, reviewable data/answer-key path can support the bounded task.

**Kill criteria**

- Any critical false unique result; any meaning-changing fact silently dropped, changed, defaulted, or inherited; stale output shown after failure; or workflow-induced belief in equivalence, suitability, listing, or approval.
- The normal method is consistently faster with equal or better correctness for more than half.
- A qualified reviewer cannot define a truthful bounded profile/stopping rule, or permitted data cannot satisfy it.

**Current verdict:** **FAIL at this gate; retain only as a research hypothesis.** A remains the correct next test, not the correct product direction.

### B — fastener BOM specification preflight / issue queue

**Pass criteria to replace A as lead**

- A's criteria pass first; B uses exactly the same line resolver and preserves every raw row independently.
- More than half prefer B for task-specific reasons after counterbalanced use.
- For more than half, B produces a concrete corrected line, precise question, honest outside-scope decision, or usable handoff more efficiently than A.
- For more than half, B prevents more necessary follow-ups than it creates and yields a named reuse event.
- B reduces real review effort on naturally recurring action groups, not merely card openings, while inducing no readiness/approval misconception.

**Fail criteria**

- B adds only packet identity, rollup, grouping, and queue chrome while line truth and substantive review are unchanged.
- Real packets have little repeated-action density, or clean/exact packets are burdened by queue bookkeeping.
- Preference, downstream use, or follow-up reduction does not clear the more-than-half thresholds.

**Kill criteria**

- Any group action suppresses row identity, bulk-applies a mechanical choice, hides a conflict/failure, or creates a false readiness/approval interpretation.
- B creates the same or more follow-up work than the participant's normal method for more than half.

**Current verdict:** **FAIL as A's replacement.** The 10-line fixture produced eight groups, no new truth, no fewer substantive reviews, and no direct-user evidence. Retain only a reversible issue-packet wrapper if real repeated groups later justify it.

### C — category-scoped specification integrity / broader mechanical direction

**Pass criteria as a future direction**

- A separately approved category/job schema, typed target objects, lawful evidence, and qualified domain review exist; no fastener completion predicate is transferred unchanged.
- Direct cases in both fasteners and at least one contrasting category show that the shared integrity packet safely changes a real next action.
- Identity, manufacturer execution, seller-neutral description, application requirements, and suitability remain separate; category-specific stopping rules are explicit.
- Repeated evidence, not one non-killing bearing case, supports a shared product value proposition.

**Fail criteria**

- The shared layer is only generic preserve/freeze/abstain mechanics while the useful job immediately returns to a manufacturer or specialist.
- A neutral configuration erases manufacturer execution or conflates product decoding with application selection.
- One contrasting case is used as category coverage or platform evidence.

**Kill criteria**

- Any meaning-changing designation token is dropped, a confident wrong manufacturer execution is produced, or the workflow induces equivalence, replacement, or application-suitability belief.
- The useful endpoint necessarily depends on manufacturer-specific evidence/calculation and the intermediate packet does not change the participant's next action.

**Current verdict:** **FAIL as the POC direction.** The unchanged fastener model is already falsified for rolling bearings. Preserve only the smaller truth-operation kernel for later research; no bearing ingestion or platform claim.

### D — stop/rethink

**Pass criteria for choosing D**

- Any A, B, or C safety kill occurs; or
- fewer than five qualifying sessions can be completed safely; or
- fewer than half find a useful bounded artifact/changed next action; or
- the normal method wins with equal or better correctness for more than half; or
- lawful data plus qualified mechanical review cannot produce a truthful bounded release/stopping rule.

**Fail criteria for choosing D**

- A clears every direct-task, safety, comprehension, data, and domain criterion with contrary cases retained. Only then is stopping the bounded direction not supported.

**Kill criteria requiring immediate D**

- Evidence must be invented, participants simulated, confidential work retained improperly, unsafe mechanical authority claimed, or unresolved formal approval bypassed to keep the project moving.

**Current verdict:** Apply D **now to specification, production, release, supplier-handoff, and platform work**. Do not yet abandon one final bounded manual A study: its execution gate has been blocked rather than run. If participants, recent work, independent keys, and a qualified reviewer cannot be secured, stop the direction.

## Disagreements and unresolved questions

1. **Earlier synthesis says “choose A now”; this gate says A is only the next test.** The synthesis was directional desk judgment. Zero direct sessions and zero formal mechanical approvals prevent upgrading it to a product decision.
2. **The benchmark says 120/120; the domain review says release-blocking answer-key and projection defects exist.** Both can be true: the composer matches its own contract while that contract is mechanically incomplete or wrong. The unfinished independent adjudication means the defects remain unresolved; the conservative decision is to block release use.
3. **B's 12/12 artifact score conflicts with any implication that B is a better product.** It measures declared structure only. Identical line truth, ten substantive reviews in both conditions, and zero participants resolve the product question against replacement now.
4. **Prototype 006 looks safe while the current runtime is unsafe.** The fixture passes its designated states; the runtime fails three of four exercised block decisions. A fixture cannot authorize production handoff.
5. **The authoritative contract describes generic search and supplier destinations as current capabilities.** The same contract says implementation mismatch is a defect, not permission to expand claims. Runtime evidence therefore blocks reliance on those capabilities for the new direction.
6. **Bearing work preserves a kernel but rejects the platform center.** Preserve/type/freeze/abstain operations survive; a universal neutral configuration and alternate-supplier path do not.
7. **The domain family review is detailed but not formal approval.** It can block unsafe acceptance; it cannot approve a family. The named approver, authority, lawful normative access, and signed release digest remain unresolved.
8. **Evidence integrity is not fully closed.** Two empirical review files remain pending skeletons, and the engineer-study audit contains a stale description of the now-completed A/B fixture report. The gate relies only on completed, internally bounded claims and treats unresolved contradictions conservatively.
9. **Minimum sample wording differs.** The decision ticket asks for 6–8; the ready protocol permits five as the minimum bounded round. Use five as the minimum count and aim for 6–8; do not pass with fewer.
10. **Unknown commercial question:** no evidence establishes buyer, budget, repeat behavior, or willingness to pay. Passing safety mechanics would still not by itself establish a business.

## Findings under the required classification vocabulary

### Evidence

- Internal fixtures support fail-closed composition and packet mechanics, but the current runtime/projection fails major safety and release-aware checks.
- Direct participant count is zero and formal mechanical approvals are zero.
- The candidate release is blocked; the unchanged fastener model fails the bearing transfer; B adds structure without demonstrated product value.

### Fundamental requirement

- Keep technical state-machine evidence, direct-user value evidence, and formal mechanical approval as independent gates. None may substitute for another.
- Require participant-owned recent work, independent keys, qualified domain review, immutable release identity for release claims, and the safety criteria above before `/to-spec`.

### Strong hypothesis

- A compact frozen issue packet may improve specification recovery and handoff without requiring supplier magic.
- A thin batch wrapper may help only when real packets contain repeated next-safe-action groups.

### Opportunity / gold idea

- Test the smallest durable unit: preserved clue + typed interpretation + unresolved facts + safe stop + correction-aware packet.
- Make a successful unresolved issue packet a legitimate endpoint rather than forcing selection or supplier navigation.

### Nice-to-have

- Differences-only review, print layout, keyboard mode, and local reuse aids after safety, comprehension, and repeat value pass.

### Open question

- Does the packet beat the participant's normal source/catalog workflow and change a real next action?
- Can qualified review and lawful data support even one bounded family/release, and will participants reuse or pay for the result?

### Risk

- Contract-green fixtures, polished queues, exact identifiers, or release language can create false authority despite no mechanical approval or user value.
- Continuing prototypes without participants can convert research motion into false confidence.

### Rejected

- GO to `/to-spec`; B replacing A now; the current candidate release; supplier translation as core completion; BOM-readiness claims; bearing ingestion; broad platform claims; production edits; and treating internal agents, fixtures, desk reviews, or source examples as users.
