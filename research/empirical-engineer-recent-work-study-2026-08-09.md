## 5. Roles, blinding, and answer-key separation

Use three roles when feasible:

1. **Mechanical/domain reviewer:** receives only the redacted technical packet and prepares the answer key before the session. The reviewer checks each explicit fact, permissible mapping, ambiguity/conflict, category boundary, and safe stopping point. The reviewer does not design A/B copy and does not observe participant preference before freezing the key.
2. **Moderator:** receives the participant task deck plus a sealed scoring copy of the frozen key. The moderator does not alter truth to rescue a prototype and does not reveal expected states before locked comprehension scoring.
3. **Analyst:** codes anonymized observations after the session against the frozen key. Any post-session key correction is versioned, justified, and causes affected scores to be recalculated; it is never silently edited.

If one person must fill two roles, disclose the limitation and preserve the procedural separation: key frozen first, participant deck contains no answers, raw observations locked before interpretation.

### Required answer-key package

Create outside the participant-facing deck:

- key ID and version;
- redacted packet digest/local reference;
- per-line raw facts and notation;
- expected interpretation state: `exact`, `partial`, `conflict`, `unsupported`, `unknown`, or `failure`;
- separate handoff disposition: `configuration-handoff-supported`, `engineering-input-required`, `outside-release`, or `processing-blocked`;
- family/category and target object, if supported;
- required versus nonblocking facts under an approved scoped profile, or an explicit statement that no such profile exists;
- expected safe stopping point;
- prohibited inferences;
- every allowed unique mapping and its source/release/review basis;
- unresolved reviewer disagreement.

A line with reviewer disagreement cannot score as a unique configuration. It must be keyed as unresolved/ambiguous or removed before the session, with removal documented. The participant never sees expected answers until all scored actions and teach-backs are locked.

## 11. Kill, stop, and decision rules

Apply safety rules before value or preference.

### Immediate session stop

Stop and do not count the affected task/session when:

- consent is absent or withdrawn;
- confidential, controlled, export-restricted, identifying, credential, or private commercial information is exposed and cannot be safely removed;
- the participant is not eligible or the artifact is not their permitted recent work;
- the moderator cannot maintain neutrality;
- completing a task would require a false equivalence, suitability, approval, listing, price, stock, availability, certification, or order claim;
- a supplier destination requires login, bot bypass, quote, cart, or purchasing action;
- the frozen answer key is absent or has unresolved disagreement for a claimed unique result.

### Evidence exclusion

Do not count a session as direct evidence if it lacks session/note consent, a safely redacted recent-work packet, a frozen independent key, the required modules, or complete observation notes. Do not repair a missing module using moderator memory, fixture behavior, or an agent simulation.

### Safety kill for the tested workflow/composition

Pause/reject the implicated workflow or wording if any of these occurs:

- any critical false unique configuration/product match;
- any selection-critical explicit fact is silently changed, dropped, defaulted, or inherited from another line;
- a service failure shows stale output as current;
- any participant makes an unsafe action because the interface implies equivalence, suitability, supplier match/listing, approval, stock, source transparency, or technical review that does not exist;
- a participant cannot locate the blocking fact or explain why the action is blocked after inspecting the available evidence.

Do not average away a safety failure. Diagnose and revise before any fresh test.

### A versus B decision

**B may replace A as the lead challenger only if all are true:**

- zero critical false unique matches in either condition;
- every raw line and explicit fact survives both conditions;
- more than half of qualifying participants prefer B for task reasons after both conditions;
- for more than half, B creates a concrete corrected line, precise engineering question, honest outside-release decision, or usable handoff that A does not provide as efficiently;
- B creates fewer necessary follow-ups than it prevents for more than half;
- more than half name a concrete next comparable event for reuse;
- no participant interprets B as procurement-, production-, approval-, equivalence-, or suitability-ready.

If safety passes but these value conditions do not, retain A and reject/narrow B's lead claim. If participants mostly open the original source and find A equally clear/faster, narrow A's value claim rather than manufacturing batch value.

### Current wedge kill/rethink

Stop or rethink the bounded fastener wedge if direct task evidence shows any of the following:

- the normal source/catalog method is consistently faster with equal or better keyed correctness for more than half of qualifying participants;
- fewer than half produce a useful frozen issue/configuration artifact or name a real next action it improves;
- participants' desired outcome is primarily price, live availability, approved vendor, equivalence, ERP/PLM integration, or quality/certification evidence outside the bounded product;
- no permitted participant packet and reviewer process can support truthful scoped resolution;
- fewer than five qualifying sessions can be completed safely; in that case the gate remains **blocked**, not passed or killed from invented evidence.

Because recruitment requires a recent task, this sample cannot prove population frequency. Failure to recruit enough eligible recent-work participants is a feasibility blocker and negative signal, not a prevalence estimate.

### Trust wording/composition kill

Reject the tested trust treatment if any participant acts on a critical misconception induced by the treatment, or if participants repeatedly conflate:

- `release` with approval;
- `supplied` with correct;
- `normalized` with source-authored;
- confidential lineage with independently inspectable provenance;
- exact mapping with suitability/equivalence;
- supplier query with match/listing/stock.

Preference and confidence cannot override these rules.

### Supplier-handoff kill/narrow

Remove destination-specific translation from the value claim if it produces no concrete next step for more than half, requires the same or more reformulation than the participant's normal method for more than half, or creates any unsafe match/equivalence/listing belief. Keep source-direct opening separate. A destination denial is disclosed, not treated as absence.

### Bearing/platform falsifier

Reject the current universal neutral-configuration/platform claim from the single case if any of these occurs:

- a designation token that changes meaning is dropped or normalized away;
- the workflow produces a confident wrong manufacturer product/execution;
- the packet is interpreted as an equivalent, replacement, or application-qualified selection because of the workflow;
- the useful endpoint necessarily depends on manufacturer-specific execution or application calculation outside the boundary and the intermediate packet does not change the participant's next action.

A non-kill result leaves **Open question** status. It does not validate bearing coverage, category expansion, or a mechanical platform.
