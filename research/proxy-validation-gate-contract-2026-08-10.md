# Proxy Validation Gate contract — 2026-08-10

> **HISTORICAL — COMPLETED PROXY GATE.** Preserve method and results. It does not control current product behavior, product validation, or the `/to-spec` frontier.

**Status:** Historical — inactive  
**Scope:** Research and deterministic validation only  
**Hard boundary:** Do not start `/to-spec`. Do not implement production code. Do not represent specialists as users, practicing engineers, qualified approvers, or independent release reviewers.

## Gate question

Can public technical authority, manufacturer documentation, repository evidence, and deterministic synthetic tests reduce the bounded fastener specification-recovery hypothesis enough to choose one next direction without faking human or qualified-review evidence?

## Evidence hierarchy

1. **Public primary authority:** official standards pages and official manufacturer technical catalogs. Public metadata, terminology, scope, and independently stated facts may be cited. Copyrighted standards text or tables must not be copied.
2. **Repository observation:** checked-in source data, importer/normalizer/search/release code, frozen fixtures, deterministic scripts, and exercised prototypes.
3. **Proxy/synthetic specialist analysis:** reasoned review by specialist subagents. This can expose contradictions and define conservative candidate rules. It is not human evidence, professional sign-off, standards certification, family approval, answer-key approval, release approval, or product validation.
4. **Still absent:** participant-owned recent work, direct task comparison, comprehension evidence, willingness to pay, qualified mechanical approval, and independently approved answer keys.

Every consequential result must state its evidence tier and use one classification: **Evidence**, **Fundamental requirement**, **Strong hypothesis**, **Opportunity / gold idea**, **Nice-to-have**, **Open question**, **Risk**, or **Rejected**.

## Specialist lanes

1. **Mechanical engineer proxy:** family boundaries, function/geometry distinctions, required selection fields, conflicts, missing facts, and safe stopping points.
2. **Fastener expert proxy:** identifier namespaces, standards and thread semantics, metric/imperial quantities, neighboring family exclusions, and answer-key hazards.
3. **Manufacturing/sourcing proxy:** handoff completeness, manufacturability/procurement ambiguity, supplier-query limits, and whether the packet changes a defensible next action.
4. **UX proxy:** exact/broad/partial/conflict/failure flows, visible fact preservation, correction/revision comprehension, and unsafe-action prevention.
5. **Data-quality proxy:** importer field mapping, family leakage, units, missingness, false matches, deterministic corpus defects, release/revision integrity, and reproducible aggregate tests.
6. **Architecture/security proxy:** release immutability, public/private boundary, DTO allowlist, caller/abuse controls, correction/withdrawal, failure/stale-state behavior, and automation authority.

## Required tests

- family inclusion and exclusion boundaries;
- identifier namespace, mapping, ambiguity, and lifecycle;
- family-required and handoff-required fields;
- metric/imperial units, pitch/TPI, diameter, length, thread form, direction, fit/tolerance, and supplied-versus-normalized notation;
- explicit conflicts, missing critical facts, unsupported scope, withdrawal, unavailable service, and stale results;
- false unique identity and false unique selection;
- immutable release/revision, correction, supersession, withdrawal, and rollback;
- safe-handoff packet fact preservation and blocked unsafe actions;
- next-action usefulness against explicit synthetic workflow baselines only.

## Proxy kill rules

Choose **C — rethink** if any remains inherent to the bounded direction rather than repairable before specification:

- a family cannot be bounded without application or manufacturer-specific authority outside the product claim;
- a unique result can be produced after a critical fact is dropped, mutated, defaulted, conflicted, stale, or unreviewed;
- the packet implies suitability, equivalence, listing, approval, stock, price, or availability;
- the source and review model cannot support a lawful, reproducible bounded release;
- the packet adds process but no defensible synthetic next action versus preserving/opening the original source.

## Decision choices

- **A — proceed to human validation:** proxy review supports a coherent manual hypothesis, but direct task evidence remains the next required step. No implementation mandate.
- **B — bounded POC despite missing human validation:** proxy review supports a deliberately narrow build only if Jay later accepts the missing-human-evidence risk and explicitly approves the direction. `/to-spec` remains blocked in this gate.
- **C — rethink:** proxy review finds a structural truth, safety, usefulness, or lawful-release failure.

## Non-pass evidence

Agent agreement, synthetic fixtures, public examples, deterministic self-consistency, manufacturer documentation, and standards metadata cannot close the existing human-validation or qualified-approval gates. Tickets 15 and 24 remain open unless their original requirements are later changed explicitly by Jay.
