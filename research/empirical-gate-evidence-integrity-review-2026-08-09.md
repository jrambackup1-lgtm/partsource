## Method and evidence hierarchy

Reliability order used here:

1. **Direct-user evidence:** consented participant observation tied to a qualifying ledger row. None exists.
2. **Direct repository observation:** current source, frozen fixture, checked-in data row, or authoritative ticket/ledger text inspected in this review.
3. **Executable result:** independently rerun output. None was obtained in this review because the execution environment denied both attempted scorer/audit commands before execution.
4. **Fixture evidence:** constructed cases and their deterministic outcomes. Useful for contract regression; not incidence, preference, mechanical approval, or product value.
5. **Runtime evidence:** observed local/browser behavior. Useful for implementation behavior; not user evidence or supplier truth.
6. **Desk/domain evidence:** source-backed taxonomy or manufacturer/standard observations. Useful for falsification and requirements; not product validation.
7. **Narrative report assertion:** accepted only when reconciled with a higher-ranked artifact; transient external observations without a durable trace remain weak.

An evidence type can be reliable for a narrow claim and invalid for a broader one. For example, source code can reliably establish that a link is emitted while establishing nothing about whether an engineer finds it useful.

## Required adjudications

### Does 120/120 mean anything beyond self-consistency?

**No, not on present evidence.** It means the candidate evaluator reproduces the contract encoded in the frozen expected outputs across 120 constructed/source-composed inputs. That is useful regression evidence. It does not show:

- independent domain truth;
- a mechanically approved family or release;
- any correct unique engineering selection (the expected count is zero);
- superiority to current user workflow;
- production behavior;
- satisfaction of ticket 24's explicitly stated 150-case gate.

The result should be cited only as deterministic contract self-consistency. Even a successful independent rerun would prove reproducibility, not remove the circularity.

### Are the runtime supplier findings grounded?

**Partly.**

- **Grounded:** query field omission, parser default/collapse behavior, supplier-link control flow, neutral labeling, and the absence of supplier/commercial fields are corroborated by current source.
- **Not independently grounded here:** the exact live API payload, the reported browser `1/4` count, access-denial pages, and Zoro heading sample. Those observations are plausible and carefully bounded, but no durable screenshot/network trace/result fixture was provided and this review did not reproduce them.
- **No supplier truth follows:** a rendered search page or heading is not a listing, match, equivalent, compatibility result, suitability result, or commercial fact.

The core integrity conclusion survives without trusting the external pages: current source permits alternate-supplier search links after unresolved non-exact inputs, contrary to the active Wayfinder gate's requirement to block handoff on unresolved critical facts, conflict, unsupported scope, or unreviewed state.

### Does A-vs-B prove product preference?

**No.** There were no users and no preference measure. Both paths shared the same constructed packet, resolver rules, and answer states. B's lower number of review-start contexts and higher structural checklist score are fixture mechanics chosen and counted by the author. They establish only that an issue queue can group this packet and produce a more complete artifact under this rubric. They do not establish preference, faster completion, fewer errors, lower handoff effort, repeat use, or business value.

### Does category falsification support any platform claim?

**No positive platform claim.** It supports a negative boundary: a universal fastener-style configuration model is unsafe across rolling bearings. It may justify rejecting or narrowing that universal model. It does not validate PartSource as a platform, validate bearing support, or show that the proposed cross-category truth-operation kernel creates user value.

Ticket 24 requires a frozen **150-case** benchmark. The report deliberately supplies 120 because a subordinate synthesis permitted 100–150. The authoritative ticket was not revised. Therefore 120 does not satisfy the stated gate, regardless of score.

Ticket 15 separately calls for a 12–20 **task** benchmark plus direct target-user evidence. The 120-case oracle corpus is not a substitute for that direct task benchmark.

### Risk — participant threshold was weakened in the protocol
