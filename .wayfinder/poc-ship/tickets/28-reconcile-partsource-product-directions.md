## Question

After the failed POC, validation, Ticket 25, UX research, data findings, and the rejected BOM-audit pivot, what is the smallest credible PartSource product?

## Mission boundary

PartSource remains a mechanical-component discovery product.

The goal is to build and ship a credible POC using frontier-model multi-agent orchestration.

The paid manual BOM ambiguity audit is an alternative business experiment. It is not the product goal.

## Specialist lanes

Three independent specialists reviewed the repository and current Wayfinder:

1. skeptical product/job;
2. mechanical-domain and data safety;
3. modern engineering UX and agent orchestration.

Their outputs were checked against the product contract, domain language, Ticket 25 evidence, data-model validation, architecture red-team, current UI, and UX research.

## Resolution

Four product directions survive:

1. **A — bounded intent-adaptive resolver — recommended.**
2. **B — cited primary-source research resolver.**
3. **C — photo and measurement family triage.**
4. **D — one-family cited comparison workbench.**

The common product seam is:

> imperfect clue → preserved facts and agent proposals → deterministic reconciliation → family or candidate records → explicit unknowns/evidence → human choice or useful abstention

## Recommended smallest credible product

Direction A:

- one resolver screen;
- rough text and exact-identifier paths;
- three bounded agent roles: terminology interpreter, interpretation critic, and question writer;
- deterministic parsing, identity/cardinality, hard constraints, claim gates, and action gates;
- two or three neighboring fastener family profiles;
- 24–40 deliberately selected candidate records;
- human-selected discovery snapshot, never PartSource approval;
- no BOM or supplier workflow in the core loop.

## Permanent boundaries

The POC must not claim:

- correct selection or suitability;
- equivalence, alternate, replacement, or interchangeability;
- standards conformity beyond the exact source statement;
- listing, price, stock, availability, or best supplier;
- BOM/procurement readiness;
- qualified mechanical review when none exists;
- broad category coverage;
- agent confidence as truth.

Agents may propose, explain, challenge, and ask.

Agents may not create technical facts, default missing values, merge identity, resolve collisions, select the final candidate, or authorize downstream actions.

## Data boundary

Use only the minimum semantic objects:

- input clue;
- fact occurrence;
- interpretation proposal;
- POC family profile;
- immutable source record;
- POC-scoped candidate record;
- identifier clue mapping;
- frozen discovery snapshot;
- fixture/schema/parser/agent-policy versions.

Do not build trusted-release authority, a universal claim graph, EAV, a reviewer app, or a universal mechanical ontology for the POC.

## UX boundary

- one intent-adaptive resolver, not a dashboard or chatbot;
- original clue and interpretation ledger first;
- compact aligned candidate rows and differences;
- explicit conflict, unknown, not-indexed, unsupported, and unavailable states;
- no agent theater, confidence badges, generic schematics, random cards, or supplier-logo commerce;
- desktop and 320 px mobile composition.
