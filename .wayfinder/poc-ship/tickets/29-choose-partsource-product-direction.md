## Decision

Choose the product direction for the next PartSource behavioral POC.

This decision is closed. Ticket 30 is the current Wayfinder frontier.

The mission is fixed:

> Build and ship a credible PartSource mechanical-component discovery POC using frontier-model multi-agent orchestration.

The decision is product shape, not whether PartSource becomes a manual BOM-audit service.

## Choices

### A — bounded intent-adaptive resolver — recommended

One resolver accepts rough language, partial specifications, standards, and known identifiers.

It preserves explicit facts, asks three bounded agents to propose/challenge interpretations, reconciles deterministically, shows family or candidate records, exposes unknowns/evidence, and lets the engineer select or abstain.

Best balance of:

- mission fit;
- visible agent leverage;
- deterministic safety;
- current research reuse;
- one coherent buildable loop.

Primary risk: narrow fixture coverage may feel staged and real user value remains unvalidated.

### B — cited primary-source research resolver

Agents search an allowlisted evidence corpus or official source set, challenge identity and conflicts, and compose a cited research brief.

Best visible multi-agent orchestration.

Primary risks: source permission, retrieval drift, latency, prompt injection, and larger implementation scope.

### C — photo and measurement family triage

Vision agents propose possible component families, a critic states what the image cannot establish, and deterministic family rules ask for discriminating measurements.

Most differentiated and demo-friendly.

Primary risks: hidden mechanical facts, broad domain scope, and hobbyist rather than engineering demand.

### D — one-family cited comparison workbench

A deterministic family workbench compares nearby records by consequential differences, missing facts, and evidence. Agents assist only with anomaly and explanation work.

Safest and easiest to benchmark.

Primary risks: looks like a polished small catalog and underuses the multi-agent mission.

### E — revise named elements

Change or combine only explicitly named elements before approving a direction.

Do not merge all four.

## Recommended choice

Choose **A**.

Use these elements only:

- one intent-adaptive resolver;
- rough-text and exact-identifier paths;
- terminology interpreter, interpretation critic, and question writer agents;
- deterministic parsing, identity/cardinality, hard constraints, claim gates, and action gates;
- two or three neighboring fastener family profiles;
- 24–40 deliberately selected permission-cleared or clearly synthetic candidate records;
- user-selected frozen discovery snapshot;
- useful abstention as a successful completion.

Do not add BOM, supplier search, photo input, live unrestricted browsing, broad catalog publication, or release-authority machinery to the first loop.

## Shared promise

> Turn an imperfect mechanical-component clue into inspectable candidate families or records, show what is known and unknown, and help you decide what to verify next.

## Shared prohibited claims

No direction may claim:

- correct component selection;
- suitability;
- equivalence, alternate, replacement, or interchangeability;
- approval or certification;
- listing, price, stock, availability, or best supplier;
- BOM or procurement readiness;
- qualified review when none exists;
- broad platform coverage from a narrow fixture;
- agent confidence as truth.
