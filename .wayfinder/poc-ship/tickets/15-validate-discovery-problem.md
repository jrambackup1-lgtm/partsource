## Why this remains open

Close only after 6–8 qualifying recent-work sessions, qualified mechanical review, and a corrected independently reviewable 150-case benchmark establish A, B, or stop.

1. Recent-work study with 6–8 engineers and their own redacted lines.

3. Manual 5–15-line BOM-readiness concierge duel.

Run A one line at a time and B as a manual issue queue on the same recent redacted inputs. Internal fixtures did not establish a winner: B added structure but no line truth. A remains the least-unsupported next manual test. Keep this ticket open and do not start `/to-spec`.
