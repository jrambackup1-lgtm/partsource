## Question

Which high-friction jobs across mechanical-part specification, identification, reuse, procurement handoff, BOM work, and supplier discovery are real, frequent, and valuable enough for PartSource to solve? Which opportunities would make the product materially better than a supplier catalog or query generator?

## Findings

Report: `research/full-problem-space-product-opportunities-2026-08-09.md`.

Configuration recovery survives as an entry hook, not the whole wedge. Requirement continuity is the leading value hypothesis. A 5–15-line BOM readiness audit is the strongest challenger. Direct user frequency, reuse, and willingness-to-pay evidence are still absent.

## Resolution — 2026-08-10

Closed as a completed product-jobs/opportunity synthesis. Demand, frequency, reuse, and willingness-to-pay remain unvalidated and stay owned by Tickets 15 and 24.
