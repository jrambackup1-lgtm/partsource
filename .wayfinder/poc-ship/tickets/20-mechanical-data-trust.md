## Question

## Findings

Report: `research/mechanical-data-trust-opportunities-2026-08-09.md`.

A small reviewed release, family identity profiles, typed quantities when needed, separate identifier evidence, field-group provenance, and immutable release context are leading directions. Mechanical review and normalization decisions remain open.
